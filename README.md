# Torched Planet

A turn-based artillery duel built with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript — an homage to Scorched Earth. Play as the left tank against a CPU opponent across randomized salt flats, hills, and city rooftops.

[Play it here](https://torched-planet.vercel.app)

## Features
* **Endless Progression:** Duel, win, and upgrade your tank as the game difficulty scales.
* **Artistic Pixel Art:** A hand-crafted, retro look that works on desktop and mobile.
* **Deterministic Physics:** Precise ballistics for both you and your opponent.
* **Tactical Economy:** Manage your wallet to buy shells, fuel, and hull upgrades.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aim turret | Left / Right or A / D | Tap ◀ ▶, hold to sweep |
| Change power | Up / Down or W / S | Tap ▲ ▼, hold to ramp |
| Fine aim | Hold Shift | Tap: exact 1% / 1° steps |
| Move tank | Z / X | Hold L / R |
| Fire | Space | Tap Fire |
